Public media resolver

A clean online player for public video URLs.

Just Watch finds playable sources published by public media pages and opens them in a focused browser player. Paste a link, press Watch, and choose an available MP4, HLS, or MPEG-DASH source without navigating through source-page pop-ups.

Resolve public media

The layered resolver checks direct media links, standard video metadata, safe same-origin player APIs, supported site adapters, and a broad yt-dlp fallback.

Play common formats

Use Just Watch as an online MP4 player, HLS player, or DASH player when the source is public, unprotected, and compatible with browser playback.

Keep the flow simple

The application does not store a local URL history or host the full video file. Playback is requested from the original media host or CDN.

FAQ

Questions about Just Watch

Is Just Watch a video hosting service?

No. Just Watch resolves source information and lets the browser request media from its original host. It does not upload or permanently store the video file.

Does Just Watch remove advertisements from videos?

It avoids opening source-page pop-ups and does not inject advertisements. It does not remove ads already embedded inside a video or live stream.

Which links can Just Watch open?

It is intended for public, unprotected URLs. DRM, login requirements, paywalls, private content, CAPTCHA, region locks, and source-specific restrictions are not bypassed.
